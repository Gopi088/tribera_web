## Remaining actionable issues (none blocking, listed by severity)

**Minor / worth a manual check, not confirmed as bugs:**
- On every route, the mono breadcrumb ("ALL ROLES / ENGINEERING," etc.) and the faded secondary nav row sit very close in value to the black hero background. At the screenshot compression used here (up to ~5x downscale on the 1520px shots), I can't rule out this being simply the intended muted/restrained mono label treatment rather than a contrast problem. Recommend a live-browser contrast check rather than treating this as confirmed.
- Tag chips under each hero H1 (role/type/location/experience) are small, muted-gray-on-dark mono text. Legible in every screenshot I read, consistent with the "restrained" style, but if there's a WCAG contrast target, worth a quick automated contrast check — I'm not confirming a failure, just flagging it hasn't been measured.

**Not defects (confirming disposition's "not treated as bugs" still holds):**
- No FAQ/accordion content exists on these five pages — the "COLLAPSIBLE CONTENT" copy-file marker is simply empty because these pages have no collapsibles, not a missing-answer bug (that finding was specific to Coverage pages).
- The two-step apply flow (email field → "CV and paragraph on the next screen") is a deliberate progressive-disclosure design per the copy, not a dead/broken CTA — I did not test the actual submission behavior since that's functional, not visual/copy.

**No overflow, clipping, truncated copy, obsolete promises, or inconsistent card shapes found** across desktop or mobile renders for any of the five routes.
