This is inconsistent with the Fraunces editorial heading convention used everywhere else on the site (see baseline). Likely the tag-label formatting isn't title-casing single-token slugs. Confirmed visually and in copy captures across both breakpoints — not speculative.

**Low / non-issues (per disposition, not to be re-raised as bugs)**
- Card counts of 1 article on empathy, engineering, hiring-insights, hiring-metrics vs. 2 on decision-infrastructure are correct data-driven states, not a layout defect.
- Filter pill sets on each tag page do not list every tag a card is filed under — expected per the taxonomy note in the disposition, not a bug.

**Owner-evidence required (not a design/code blocker)**
- The hiring-insights article teaser cites "research from McKinsey, Deloitte, and PwC" — sourcing/permission for these named-research claims is unverified by this review, consistent with the disposition's owner-evidence list.

No unreadable text, overflow, broken cards, or dead-looking controls found on any of the five routes at either breakpoint. Only the tag-casing inconsistency needs a fix before calling this tranche fully resolved.
