## Findings by severity

**Blocker (visual/code)**
- `/candidates/start/`: "Submit profile" button uses a light pink fill instead of the site's standard solid red CTA color. At a glance it looks disabled, which will suppress conversions on the one page whose entire job is to collect a submission. Recommend matching the red used on `/candidates/` "Create your profile" and `/careers/ai-ml-engineer/` "Apply."

**High (visual/UX)**
- `/careers/`: Hero scorecard shows an empty "ROLE" dropdown with every dimension at 0%, with no selected role and no visible instruction to pick one. Combined with the fact that no role list/cards appear later on the page despite the "7, across 3 teams" headline, a first-time visitor has no clear path to actually see or click into an open role from this page. Needs either a populated default role or a visible role grid under "Open roles."
- `/candidates/start/`: The CV upload dropzone's placeholder text is very low-contrast (light grey on light grey) at both breakpoints — borderline unreadable, especially on mobile.

**Medium**
- `/careers/` mobile (390px): the same 0%-everywhere scorecard appears compressed under the hero copy; on a narrow viewport it reads even more like inert/broken UI since there's less surrounding context to signal it's illustrative.

**Low / copy-owner verification (not a design blocker)**
- `/candidates/`: Client logo list (Mizuho, Capco, Deloitte, ArcelorMittal, Flipkart, Myntra, Motherson, Publicis Sapient, Publicis Resources, Evident Microscopy, Antz) is a factual claim — flag for owner confirmation of current/permitted logo usage, separate from any code fix.
- `/candidates/`: Sample profile ("Anirudh Mehta," 91.1 score) and CV/interview comparison examples are explicitly labeled "illustrative/sample" in copy — good practice, no action needed, noting only because it's adjacent to the logo claim above and worth keeping distinct (fictional example vs. real client claim).

## Not flagged (checked, no issue)
- Testimonials on `/candidates/` and legal/process substance on `/careers/` and `/careers/ai-ml-engineer/` are intact, attributed, and unedited from what the copy files show.
- No text overflow, truncation, or broken wrapping found in either blog post or the two careers pages at 390px.
- Typography system (Fraunces headings, Inter Tight body, mono eyebrow labels) and color palette (white/paper/charcoal, restrained red) are consistently applied across all five routes except the one CTA noted above.

**Summary:** 4 of 5 routes match the approved style with no blocking issues; `/candidates/start/` has a CTA color bug that needs a code/design fix, and `/careers/` has a UX gap around the empty scorecard and missing visible role list that's worth a product decision (populate a default role, and/or add the role grid the "See the roles" button implies).
