## Findings by severity

### High
1. **"NEWSLETTER IN PREPARATION — Get notified when it is ready" ships on 4 of 5 routes.** A live-looking email field and red Subscribe button for a product that the label itself says does not exist. Newest post here is dated Feb 15, 2026; as of today this promise has been "in preparation" for months with no date. Either wire it and drop the "in preparation" framing, or remove the block. Confirm whether submissions are actually captured — an unwired field on four URLs is worse than no field.
2. **Duplicated body copy across two live URLs.** Route 1's "We check the company too" and route 2's "So we check the company too" are near-identical for three paragraphs, closing on the same sentence ("pretending otherwise would be its own kind of disrespect"). Route 3 and route 4 both contain "A bar written after the first CV is a description of that CV." Rewrite one side of each pair.
3. **Two-doors CTA asymmetry on every route.** The hiring card gets a solid red button; the candidate card gets a bare underlined "Create your profile" text link. On a page pair that claims both sides are choosing, the candidate door renders as the weaker/secondary control. Same asymmetry on mobile.

### Medium
4. **Placeholder-looking graphics.** The inline figures (route 1's "toggle" and grey-pill/red-bar image, route 3's bar figures) and most related-card thumbnails are grey-bar abstractions that read as skeleton loaders or unloaded images. Users will scroll past them assuming a failed load. Route 1's second figure is the worst offender.
5. **Route 3's unlabelled chart carries a statistical claim.** The caption asserts "the strongest correlation was whether the bar existed in writing before sourcing began… candidate quality… barely moved the number at all" over bars with no axis, no n, no scale. Either label it or demote it to a non-chart device.
6. **Audience toggle appears stuck on "I'm hiring."** On all five pages, including the two candidate-audience articles, the "I'm hiring" pill appears selected and "I'm looking" greyed. If this is a persisted preference rather than a page-context default, candidate readers land in the wrong mode. (Selected-vs-hover state is my reading of a low-res pill; verify in browser.)
7. **Related module is effectively a fixed list.** Routes 2, 4 and 5 all surface the identical trio (Rethinking / Designing for trust / Designing AI). "Designing AI" appears in all five; "Hiring is not a transaction" appears in none. The section is headed "Three more that came out of real mandates" — it reads as curated but behaves as a fallback.
8. **Share and tag controls look dead.** "SHARE · LinkedIn · Copy link" is tiny mono text with no button or icon affordance; the tag chips are outlined pills that look clickable but I cannot confirm they link anywhere. Both need either real affordance or de-emphasis.

### Low
9. Tag chips wrap to a second row on routes 3, 4 and 5, orphaning one chip beneath a right-aligned SHARE cluster. Reserve the row or move SHARE below.
10. Hero eyebrow (date · category · read time) is very small red/grey mono on near-black across all five; suspected contrast and legibility problem, not measured.
11. Footer "BENGALURU" bottom-right is near-invisible dark-grey on black.
12. "Belong. Build. Become." appears three times per page (article sign-off, large lockup, footer brand block).
13. Editorial tic: three of five articles hinge on "the thing we had backwards / got wrong for two years"; route 3 uses it as both sub-headline and an H2.
14. Route 1's H1 "Designing for trust" doesn't match its slug (`…-privacy-transparency-respect`), and its related-card dek is a truncation of a different sentence.
15. Straight apostrophe in route 4 ("hiring managers' willingness") against curly quotes site-wide.
16. Header primary CTA is "Start with one role" but the in-page hiring CTA is "See how one role ran" — two different primary next steps for the same audience on the same page.

## Claims needing owner evidence (not design blockers)
- **"What 200,000 interviews taught us"** (route 3 hero sub) — the largest number on the blog, unsourced.
- **"three interviews per hire is roughly where a calibrated process settles"** and the correlation caption (route 3) — needs the underlying mandate data or softer framing.
- **Company-side vetting**: funded headcount, number of rounds, decision speed, manager tenure, and *"did the last few people hired through us stay"* (routes 1 and 2) — asserts both an operating capability and a placement-tenure dataset. Confirm it exists and that clients contractually supply it.
- **Candidate privacy guarantees** (route 1): not searchable, blocked companies "never see that you exist," companies cannot message you, candidate sees the identical score the hiring team sees, "anonymous until you decide otherwise," "you keep the score." These are legal-grade commitments — confirm each against the shipped product and the privacy policy. Preserve them only if all are currently true.
- **Service SLAs repeated on all five routes**: "A 45-minute intake call. Three names back inside 36 hours," "A hundred signals," "Fifteen minutes to set up."
- **"A 327-profile search that closed in 22 days"** (related card, route 3).
- **"Arjun"** (route 5): 140 applications, three replies, three teams inside 48 hours. If real, confirm consent; if composite, say so.
- **Bylines**: "twenty years of enterprise hiring" (route 3), "built the signal engine" (route 1).
- **"We never share the list"** (newsletter, 4 routes) — a data promise attached to a list that may not yet exist.

Scope note: this covers only the five blog article routes listed. I did not review /blog/index, the linked "Designing AI" and "Why tech hiring" posts, or any Two-doors/footer destination.
