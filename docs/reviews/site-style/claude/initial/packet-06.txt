## Findings by severity

### High
1. **Corrupted copy on `/functions/data-analytics/`, Q1 "thin answer":** "no attзolution" — contains a stray Cyrillic "з" (should read "attribution"). This is visible, broken English on a public page.
2. **Second corruption, same page, Q3 "evidence to listen for":** "a dashthat lies says all four unprompted" — missing word, reads as "dashthat" (should be "a dashboard that lies," or similar). Both errors are in the same section, suggesting a find/replace or encoding mishap that wasn't proofread.
3. **Weighting-bar math doesn't sum to 100% for the "Data & analytics" function**, in two places: the `/functions/` comparison widget (Function B column) and the bar chart on `/functions/data-analytics/` itself. Values given: Core depth 24 + Systems design 20 + Controls and risk 10 + Delivery speed 16 + Documentation 18 + Stakeholder handling 14 = **102%**, not 100. By contrast, the Engineering & Platform side sums correctly to 100% in both places. Since this is the site's core "look how rigorous our weighting is" proof point, a visible arithmetic error undermines the exact claim it's trying to make. This is a data/content bug, not a design one — flagging for the content owner to correct the source numbers.

### Medium
4. **Prose/chart label mismatch on `/functions/data-analytics/`:** the callout text says "the column next to it — analytical judgment — is the one that separates dashboards from decisions," but the actual bar chart column is labeled "Systems design," not "analytical judgment." Reads as a copy edit that was never reconciled with the chart labels.
5. **Named individuals with specific performance stats on `/functions/engineering-platform/`** ("Pratik — 11 years, owned a payments platform at 200 req/s"; "Meera…"; "Raghav…"; "Archana…") — flagging as a **claim needing owner evidence/consent**: (a) confirm these are real people who've consented to public naming, and (b) "200 req/s" is a modest number to hold up as a scale flex for a "payments platform" — worth double-checking it's the intended stat and not a typo (e.g., missing a "k"). Note the sibling `/functions/data-analytics/` bench section does **not** name individuals, so there's also a stylistic inconsistency between the two function pages worth resolving one way or the other.
6. **Grammar on `/functions/`:** "a 8-point swing" should be "an 8-point swing."

### Low
7. **Interactive-affordance inconsistency:** `/contact/`'s FAQ accordion uses a clear red `+` marker per row (obviously clickable). The "Three things teams ask first" lists on both function detail pages use a plain small triangle/arrow glyph with no visible card/border treatment — from the screenshot alone these read as flatter, less "clickable" than the contact page's equivalent pattern. Can't confirm from a static image whether they actually expand; flagging only the visual inconsistency in affordance design between two components that appear to serve the same purpose.
