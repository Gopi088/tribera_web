## Findings by severity

**High**
- **Broken/inconsistent filter control.** The copy dump on every one of the 5 pages contains a full taxonomy list (All, Candidate Experience, Technical Hiring, AI & Assessment, Hiring Philosophy, Hiring Strategy, talent-acquisition, decision-infrastructure), implying a tag-filter pill row should render. Visually, 4 of 5 pages (empathy, engineering, hiring-insights, hiring-metrics) show **no filter pills at all**. Only decision-infrastructure shows **one** red pill, and it just repeats the current tag name — it isn't a filter set, it's a dead-looking single button with no visible function. This reads as a broken component, not a design choice.
- **Unfinished-looking grid layout.** The card grid appears built for 2+ columns, but 4 of the 5 tag pages have exactly one post, leaving a large empty column to the right of the single card (desktop only — mobile stacks fine). This looks unfinished/broken rather than intentional, especially since decision-infrastructure (which happens to have 2 posts) fills the grid correctly by contrast.

**Medium**
- **Excessive dead whitespace above the fold.** On both desktop and mobile, there's a large blank gap between the hero dek ("Notes on hiring, assessment and the candidate experience.") and the first card — roughly half the viewport on desktop, and on mobile it pushes the only content below the fold, forcing a scroll through empty space before any real content appears.
- **Slug leaking into headline.** The Fraunces H1 on every tag page is the raw URL slug verbatim ("decision-infrastructure," "hiring-insights," "hiring-metrics") rather than a human-readable title ("Decision Infrastructure," "Hiring Insights"). This breaks the otherwise polished editorial tone used elsewhere on the site.
- **No way back / no post count.** None of the 5 pages show a "back to all posts" link or any indication of how many posts exist under the tag, so a visitor landing on a single-post tag page has no path to browse further other than global nav.

**Low**
