## Findings by severity

**Blocker (design/code — needs engineering fix)**
1. **Tag filter chips are broken across all 5 routes.** The copy dump for every route lists "All / Candidate Experience / Technical Hiring / AI & Assessment / Hiring Philosophy / Hiring Strategy / talent-acquisition / decision-infrastructure" as filter chips, but on 4 of 5 pages they render as nothing — just a large blank gap (roughly 300–400px tall on desktop) between the subtitle and the post card. On `/tag/talent-acquisition/`, one chip ("talent-acquisition") escapes into the top-right of the hero as an isolated red pill unrelated to any other UI element. This reads as a rendering/positioning bug, not a design choice — it wastes vertical space above the fold and makes the filter control unusable/absent.
2. **Inconsistent card layout within the same grid** on `/tag/signal-based-hiring/` and `/tag/structured-evaluation/`. First card uses a horizontal split (image left, text right); the second card in the same row uses a vertical split (image on top, text below). This violates "aligned columns / consistent cards" and looks like a template mismatch rather than intentional variation.
3. **Broken masonry grid on `/tag/talent-acquisition/`.** Four cards render in an uneven staggered layout: the first card has a large image that leaves substantial dead whitespace beneath its tag pills, and the fourth card ("Hiring Is Not a Transaction — It's a Relationship") sits alone in the left column with no aligned partner in the right column at that row — breaks the "aligned columns" requirement.
