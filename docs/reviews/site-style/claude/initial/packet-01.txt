## Blockers (fix before ship)

1. **`/blog/` chip counts include a post that isn't in the list.** Chips read All **6** / Hiring strategy **1** / Technical hiring **2** / Candidate experience **2** / AI and assessment **1** (sums to 6). The list below has **5** rows. The missing one is the featured card, *Rethinking hiring metrics* — which is the *only* Hiring strategy post. So "Hiring strategy (1)" very likely filters to an empty list, and "All (6)" renders 5. Static screenshots can't prove the JS behaviour; the count/row arithmetic is provable and wrong either way.
2. **Read time contradicts itself.** Index: `Designing AI for high-stakes assessment — 10 MIN`. The post header: `5 MIN READ`.
3. **Index deck for that post is from a different article.** Index subtitle: "Architecture, code quality, system design." The article covers what the engine may/may not do, a rubric that was wrong, and explainability. Nothing about code quality or system design.
4. **Newsletter CTA contradicts the newsletter.** Blog hero offers "Get the next one by email"; the same page's band says "NEWSLETTER IN PREPARATION … we will send one note when the field notes newsletter opens." Two live email captures (`Notify me` on index, `Subscribe` on post) for a product that isn't open — obsolete promise plus a probable dead control. Same form, two button labels.
5. **Broken sentence, home.** "Tell us your priorities sit differently, and this is what we recalibrate." Missing the conditional ("If your priorities sit differently, tell us — this is what we recalibrate to.").

## Major

7. **`/blog/` filter chips: centered on desktop, left-aligned on mobile.** Both break the left-heading rule on desktop; at minimum they must agree across breakpoints.
