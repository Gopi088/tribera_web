### Confirmed actionable findings

**1. (Release blocker — accessibility/contrast) Industries page, "The method is the same in all twelve" dark section — step numbers 01–04 and body text.**
- Evidence: `industries-1520-section-2.png` and `industries-390-section-2.png`.
- The numerals (01/02/03/04), step titles, and descriptions render in a dark, low-contrast gray against the near-black background — visibly fainter than the equivalent white/light-gray bullet lists on the "Operators, not recruiters" dark sections of the four function pages (e.g. `functions__leadership-1520.png`). Reproduced identically at both breakpoints, so this is a genuine styling regression on this specific section, not a screenshot artifact.
- Recommendation: raise the foreground color of the step numbers/copy in this component to match the contrast level used in the "Operators, not recruiters" bullet lists elsewhere on the site.
