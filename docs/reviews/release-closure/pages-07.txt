### Actionable findings

**1. Release blocker — copy/heading mismatch**
- Route: `/industries/banking-financial-services/`
- Element: "BEFORE YOU BRIEF US" section heading
- Text: heading reads "**Three things teams ask first**" but four questions are listed immediately below and in the matching FAQ accordion: *"Do your experts actually have BFSI backgrounds?", "Can you cover both business and technology roles?", "How do you handle confidential mandates?", "What is a realistic time to offer?"*
- Evidence: `industries__banking-financial-services-copy.txt` lines 184–191 and 214–220; visible in both `-1520.png` and `-390.png` under the "BEFORE YOU BRIEF US" eyebrow.
- Every other reviewed route (ai-data, consumer-digital-commerce, cybersecurity, enterprise-technology) has exactly 3 questions matching a "Three things" heading — this route is the outlier.
- Recommendation: either change the heading to "Four things teams ask first" or drop one question (most likely the confidential-mandates one, since it's unique to this sector and could instead be folded into the backgrounds answer). This is a straightforward text fix, no layout change needed.

No other copy or visual defects were found across the five routes. Numbering/counts, weight totals, guarantee language, and section-heading alignment (left-aligned except hero and final CTA) all check out.
