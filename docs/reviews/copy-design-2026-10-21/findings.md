## Findings filtered or corrected after Claude's review

- **Candidate signup's pale button is not a confirmed defect.** `src/pages/candidates/start.astro` intentionally disables it until a CV is selected; its change handler enables it. Do not change it to “opens soon.” No profile was submitted during this review.
- **Blog's waitlist is not an undisclosed inert newsletter form.** The page explicitly says “Newsletter in preparation” and “Get notified when it is ready”; its form targets the `site-inquiry` handler. Claude overlooked that distinction. The confirmed issue is inconsistent journeys across surfaces, not absence of disclosure. Hosted delivery was not tested.
- **The AI & Data breadcrumb does not wrap at 390px.** The supplied crop shows it on one line. Claude's assertion that the same wrap affects both sampled sectors, and hence all twelve, is not supported. Review long sector names individually; do not globally truncate them on this evidence.
- **Keep readable legal line lengths.** Empty desktop space beside a narrow reading column is not itself a defect. A trust-navigation sidebar is optional and lower priority than the actual journey issues above.
- **Do not add “on the homepage” to every internal CTA.** An internal link beside an anchor link is normal; clearer action labels are useful when they explain a meaningful next step.
- **Repeated client logos and a concise sample page are not confirmed problems.** Avoid adding visual complexity merely to match long interactive pages.
- Claude flagged mobile diagram density based on downscaled full-page images. This is a candidate for targeted legibility review, not a confirmed failure from those images.
