Home full-page visual review — initial compiled Chromium artifacts

Scope: all ten Home images (handoff and migrated pairs) in artifacts/v9-g2/pages-chromium at widths 390, 768, 1024, 1366 and 1920. Read-only review; no tracked edits. These images precede the final contrast/phone keyboard fixes and do not certify those changes.

Method: sharp resized each image to 390px wide (390 source) or 600px wide (other sources), joined handoff LEFT / migrated RIGHT, and extracted consecutive 1800px-high crops. All 20 resulting crops were visually inspected with view_image, covering header through footer with no omitted vertical region: /tmp/home-review-390-{0..6}.png, 768-{0..4}, 1024-{0..2}, 1366-{0..2}, 1920-{0..1}. Source/migrated original heights: 390=11389/11415; 768=10566/10521; 1024=8740/8801; 1366=9620/9652; 1920=9731/9763. Height similarity alone is not evidence of pixel equivalence.

Verdict: no new blocking visual defect found in these initial screenshots. All ten sections remain present, readable and in the intended order. No gross horizontal overflow, section overlap, collapsed SVG, missing typography, broken image placeholder, clipped main heading or missing CTA was visible. This is a qualitative review, not a full pixel-diff acceptance or a functional test.
