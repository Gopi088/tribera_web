# Verdict: **mostly consistent** — confidence **high** for the shell/typeface/brand layer, **medium** for body-level claims (top folds only until the mid-page crops arrive)

The spine holds everywhere: 228/228 renders use a white shared navbar, 228/228 H1s are Fraunces, `--red: #da0007` is identical in `sample.css:9` and `EditorialPage.astro:23`, `--wrap: 1160px` matches in both, and the Fraunces / Inter Tight / JetBrains Mono triad is used for the same three roles (display / UI / metadata) on every family. What does **not** carry is (a) the v126 homepage sits outside the shared type-role system, (b) the eyebrow — the single most repeated v126 motif — is rendered four different ways, (c) the homepage ships a second footer with a different wordmark, and (d) the 27 taxonomy routes and `/404.html` are the two least-integrated families.

## 2. Intentional differences to **KEEP** (do not "fix")

- **The shared navbar, exactly as-is** on all 76 routes: shared `tribera.` logo, I'm hiring / I'm looking switch, Coverage (12 industries, 8 functions), Services, Intelligence, Blog, About, Contact, Careers, Log in, red "Start with one role" CTA, burger at mobile. Verified present in all 38 contact sheets. Breakpoint 1080 with the real burger at 900 — not a defect, and **not** to be replaced by a 4-anchor sample header.
- **Home's larger display scale**: H1 66.4px vs 53.12px, H2 48px vs 36px, hero lead 20.48px vs 18px. A homepage carrying a bigger display register than interior pages is normal marketing hierarchy. Keep the _sizes_; fix only the _typeface_ break at H3 (#2).
- **Home hero whitespace and centering** — centered hero, no side-by-side card, dot-grid field, deliberate breathing room before the "MOVE THE BAR YOURSELF" cue (`contact-390-01`, `contact-1520-01`). Marketing theatre, working as intended.
- **`/candidates` mirroring the home centered hero** (`contact-1520-03`) while `/about` `/blog` `/services` `/functions` `/industries` `/contact` use the left copy + right artefact hero. Two deliberate hero archetypes, both on-brand.
- **Dark full-bleed hero band on the 7 articles and 7 career-details** (white H1 on `#121212`) vs light heroes elsewhere — a legitimate editorial/role-detail signal.
- **Article body centered in a narrow measure** under a left-aligned full-width hero band (`contact-1520-02`, `-03`) — correct longread behaviour; do not widen to match marketing sections.
- **Legal family** (`/privacy`, `/security`, `/terms`): no eyebrow, no hero band, no CTA, H2 32px instead of 36px, single 16px body size (`contact-390-13`, `-19`; `contact-1520-13`, `-19`). Appropriate for legal documents; do not retrofit marketing chrome or force H2 parity.
- **Form-page density on `/candidates/start`** (11–14px field labels/helper text) and **data density on `/intelligence`** (54px `−9 pts` stat, 10px metadata) and `/about` (68px `200,000`). Field labels, metadata and data display are separate roles from lead prose — keep them separate.
- **Sub-12px mono micro-labels** are a _shared_ pattern, not drift: home itself renders 9.6px and 9.92px; `/about`, `/intelligence`, `/candidates/start` render 10px. Consistent — though 9.6px on `/` is the smallest text on the site and is worth an accessibility look separately from this review.
- **Stopped marquees** (`MANDATES RUN FOR Mizuho · Capco · Deloitte…` on `/`, `TEAMS WE HAVE WORKED WITH` on `/candidates`) and **clipping at the mask edge** — reduced-motion capture artefacts, not defects.
- **Function/industry-detail template repetition** — 20 routes sharing one hero + scorecard + H2 sequence is the intended systematized template, not copy-paste sloppiness.

## 4. Measurable font / brand findings

- **Typeface roles hold 100%.** 228/228 H1 = Fraunces. Display = Fraunces (H1/H2 on every family, plus pull-quotes and stat numerals), UI/prose = Inter Tight, metadata/eyebrow/meta-rows = JetBrains Mono. Declared identically in `sample.css:26-28` and `EditorialPage.astro:14-16`. **The one role that breaks is H3/card title on `/` only** (#2).
- **H1 scale, two values, by design:** 41.6/66.4/66.4px on `/` (`sample.css:104-107`, `clamp(2.6rem,5.6vw,4.15rem)`); 32/53.12/53.12px on all 75 others (`content-typography.css:6`, `clamp(32px,4.48vw,53.12px)`). No wide-viewport growth beyond 1520 on any route — the clamps top out identically at both 1520 and 1920, so there is no 1920-only regression anywhere.
- **H2 scale, three values:** 48px `/`; 36px on 45 marketing/index/detail routes; 32px on the 7 articles and 3 legal pages (`--type-article-size`, `--type-document-size`). The 36→32 step is role-appropriate; the 48 is home-only display.
- **Red is exact and universal:** `#da0007` in `sample.css:9` and `EditorialPage.astro:23`; hover `#c10006` (`EditorialPage.astro:308`); focus ring `2px solid #da0007` on both `index.astro:31` and `EditorialPage.astro:318-320`. Rendered red CTAs are visually identical on `/`, `/404.html`, `/candidates`, `/blog`, `/careers`, `/services`, `/functions/*`, `/industries/*`, `/contact`, `/intelligence`.
- **Ink ramp matches:** `#131313` ink, `#57565b` secondary in both systems (`sample.css:6-7` vs `EditorialPage.astro:21-22`). `--ink-3 #9a998f` exists only in v126; `.site-editorial` uses `#77776f`/`#bdbdb7` for the same tertiary role (`EditorialPage.astro:169-183`) — feeds #3/#10.
- **Container:** `--wrap: 1160px` identical; **effective column differs 1016 vs 1160px at ≥1520** (#11).
- **Dark surfaces agree:** `#121212` charcoal in both; dark cards on function/industry/career pages read as the same slab as the homepage dark sections.
- **Eyebrow metrics:** home 11.2px `#9a998f` + red tick; `.site-editorial` 12px in four colours; `/404.html` 8.96px. Letter-spacing `0.2em` is consistent in all three (`sample.css:145`, `EditorialPage.astro:64`).
