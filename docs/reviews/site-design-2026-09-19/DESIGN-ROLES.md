# Contextual design roles

The homepage remains the visual reference. Shared font families are Fraunces for display/story headings, Inter Tight for prose, forms and compact interactive titles, and JetBrains Mono for short metadata. Semantic HTML heading levels do not alone determine font family.

Page kickers use a readable 12px mono role. Neutral metadata uses #6b6a61 on light surfaces; accent page kickers use #da0007 on light surfaces. Decorative red slashes remain optional and independent of the text tone. Dark surfaces need their contextual light neutral or #ff6b6f accent; the brand CTA red remains #da0007. Meaningful compact homepage labels and the About ownership badge have an 11px floor, while explanatory lab key text uses 12px.

Warm paper (#f1eee7) belongs to the homepage story composition; neutral paper (#f4f3f0) belongs to editorial reading and interior content. Named surface tokens retain this intentional distinction.

The centered homepage story uses a 1160px outer container with responsive internal padding, keeping its latest sample composition. Editorial and specialist pages use a working column up to 1160px with responsive outer gutters to accommodate cards and charts. Articles use a narrower reading column; legal body copy is capped at 70ch while its title remains wider. Forms keep their own readable measures. These are distinct container roles, not interchangeable instances of one layout.

All routes use the same footer component, copy, navigation and contextual footer styling. The previous navbar items, destinations, wordmark, audience switch, Coverage, login, CTA and mobile menu remain in place. Only inactive audience text contrast and Blog active-state matching were changed.
