# G2 initial contrast and accessibility review

Reviewed the compiled five-page preview at `http://127.0.0.1:4390` on 2026-10-18. The artifact precedes the candidate client-strip wording correction. This is an independent rendered review, not the external review gate. Source changes below require the coordinator’s final rebuild before acceptance.

The existing hover audit checked 305 targets at 1366px and recorded 24 observations in `artifacts/v9-g2/contrast-initial.json`. Its limitation matters: it does not composite ancestor opacity, treats the first gradient stop as an entire background, and can observe an in-progress color transition. It also does not flag every pre-existing normal-text ratio between 3 and 4.5. This review therefore does not claim exhaustive WCAG conformance.

## Scope of repairs and regression checks

Only bottom overrides in `src/assets/styles/v9/home/enhancements.css` and `src/assets/styles/v9/editorial/behavior.css` change appearance. `tests/v9-contrast.test.mjs` covers both article citations and all four affected Home status/metadata labels at 390 and 1366px, plus normal/hover/keyboard-focus states for the sample link and citations. Its solid-background ratio helper composites nested group opacity after content/background composition. It refuses gradients rather than silently approximating them. The citation assertion also forbids a nonzero transition duration, so a settled-color-only test cannot miss the original failure.

Run `TEST_BASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:4390 BROWSER=chromium node --test tests/v9-contrast.test.mjs` against the rebuilt preview; Firefox and WebKit are supported. The initial artifact is expected to fail on Home’s attenuated metadata. No backend requests are made.

## Other observations

At 390 and 1366px, the five routes had no unnamed visible native controls in the bounded label census. The raw text-clipping evidence is in `artifacts/v9-g2/accessibility-initial.json`; collapsed FAQ answers, the closed About letter and the candidate honeypot account for their expected hidden content and are not blanket overflow waivers.

One confirmed independent keyboard defect was escalated separately: on Home at 390px, focusing Backend and pressing End selects Risk & markets but `focus({preventScroll:true})` leaves that tab outside the clipped strip (button x371–506; strip right edge x368). `home-phone-expert-focus.png` shows the invisible focused control. This review’s CSS scope does not fix that behavior; the Home owner must scroll the selected tab into view and verify the bounds. The review did not certify all screen-reader journeys or a complete accessibility audit.
