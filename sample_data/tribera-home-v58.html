\ What this is

We don’t send you people. We send you decisions.

A resume tells you who applied, not who’s good — so you interview to be sure. But when every round scores to a different bar, good people get dropped and weak ones slide through. It was never your panel; it was never having one bar. So we score every candidate against one, the same way, and show the proof behind every score.
