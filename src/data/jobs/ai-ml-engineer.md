## About the role

We are not looking for novelty. We are looking for AI that makes hiring decisions clearer, more structured, and more useful.

You will work on the models, scoring logic, and AI-assisted workflows that sit inside Tribera's product.

## What you'll own

- Build AI and ML workflows that improve screening and decision support
- Work across model output, evaluation, and product integration
- Partner with backend and product teams to keep the system explainable
- Track quality, failure modes, and useful signal over time

## What you bring

- Applied ML experience in real products
- Comfort with NLP, ranking, or decision-support systems
- Strong Python fundamentals and experience working with ML tooling in production
- Familiarity with model evaluation, experimentation, and prompt or workflow iteration
- Strong instincts around evaluation, iteration, and practical outcomes
- The ability to keep complexity under control and judgment visible

## Nice to have

- Experience with LLM-based systems, retrieval workflows, or model orchestration
- Voice or speech model experience
- Familiarity with LLM tooling and prompt evaluation
- Background in search, ranking, or classification systems

## Why this role matters

Tribera's value comes from better judgment, not more noise. This role helps make that real in the product.
