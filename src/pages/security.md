_Last updated_: May 27, 2026

We take security reports seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

## Reporting a Security Issue

If you believe you have found a security issue on tribera.ai, email us at [security@tribera.ai](mailto:security@tribera.ai?subject=Security%20report).

Please include:

- A clear description of the issue
- The affected URL, page, endpoint, or workflow
- Reproduction steps or a proof of concept
- The potential impact
- Any relevant screenshots, logs, or request details

## What to Expect

- We review reports in good faith.
- We may follow up if we need clarification or help reproducing the issue.
- If we confirm an issue, we will work to address it as appropriate.

## Scope and Boundaries

Please do not:

- Access, modify, or retain data that does not belong to you
- Attempt social engineering, phishing, physical attacks, or credential stuffing
- Degrade service availability through destructive testing or denial-of-service activity
- Publicly disclose a vulnerability before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it

## Bug Bounty

We do not currently run a public bug bounty program. If you send us a report, please do so with the understanding that there is no promise of payment or reward.

## Legal

This page is meant to provide a clear path for good-faith security reporting. It does not grant permission to test beyond what is allowed by law or our Terms.
