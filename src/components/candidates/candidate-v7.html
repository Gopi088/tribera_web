What a CV can carry, and what it can't

A CV describes what you were near.
A transcript shows what you decided.

Every line on a CV is a claim with no working shown. Thirty minutes of follow-up questions is the cheapest way anyone has found to tell the two apart.

On the CV

Built microservices handling two million requests a day.

What that shows

That he can write a sentence about microservices.

On the transcript

The trade-off he rejected, why he rejected it, and what it cost him six months later.

What that shows

That he made the call, and knows what it cost.
