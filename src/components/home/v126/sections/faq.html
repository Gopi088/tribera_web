How is this different from an agency or RPO?

An agency sends CVs and leaves the screening to you. We do the screening — every candidate is interviewed against your bar by someone who has done the job — and send only the three who clear it, each with the proof. And you pay per hire or per interview, never a percentage of the salary.

What if none of the three fit?

We go again, at no extra cost. The bar is yours — if the three don’t meet it, that’s ours to fix, not yours to settle.

How do you handle our candidate data?

It stays DPDP-aligned, encrypted, and retained on your terms — exportable or deletable on request. Scorecards use role-relevant criteria only; protected attributes stay out.

How fast can you start?

One call to set the bar, and your first shortlist lands in about 36 hours. One role or fifty — the same bar on every one.
